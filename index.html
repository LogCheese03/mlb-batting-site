    About the data

    Source

    The Lahman Baseball Database, compiled by Sean Lahman and maintained with SABR. It is free to download as CSV files. This project uses five of its tables: Batting, People, Fielding, Teams and TeamsFranchises.

    What one row is

    One row is one player's batting line for one team in one season (a "stint"). A player traded mid-season has one row per team. The file used by the site has rows and columns covering seasons (–) and franchises.

    Rows dropped and cleaning

    Seasons before are excluded ( rows) to keep the study to the modern era. Rows with zero plate appearances are dropped ( rows) because those players never batted. Missing counting stats ( cells, mostly sacrifice flies and hit-by-pitches in older records) are treated as zero. Team codes are mapped to franchises so that, for example, the Montreal Expos and Washington Nationals count as one group. rows have no fielding record and are labeled "DH / pinch hitter."

    How every number is computed

    All rates are computed from totals (for example, total hits divided by total at-bats), not as an average of individual players' rates. The same formulas run in scripts/prep_data.py and in the dashboard.
